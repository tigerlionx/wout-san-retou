# Wout San Retou: playtest guide

A short test of an unfinished game. **You are not being tested: the game is.** If something is confusing, too hard, boring or
scary in the wrong way, that is exactly what we need to hear. Stop whenever you want.

**Before you start (for the adult organising the test):** the game is fiction about gangs, with horror and gunfire (no
slow-motion kills, no gore as reward). It is meant for teenagers, but have a guardian's OK for under-18s, and have someone
trustworthy nearby to talk to afterwards. *Add the local help-line / counsellor contact here before testing with minors.*

## Play (20 to 30 minutes)

1. Double-click `Play.command` (Mac: if it is blocked, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again).
2. Play from the beginning. Controls: **W A S D** move, **Shift** run, **C** crouch, **F1** hide/show the controls list, **Z** lie down (Z again, Space or C to get up), **Space** jump, **mouse/arrow keys** look,
   **click** fire, **R** reload, **1-4** weapons, **E** use or wake someone by name, **V** first/third person, **M** music on/off.
3. **Press F9 whenever something feels wrong or great** (too hard, did not know what to do, boring, loved it). Nothing else is
   needed: the game saves the moment and where you were.
4. Stop after about 30 minutes, or when you want. Close the window.

## Questions (answer in your own words; short is fine)

1. In one sentence: what is this game about?
2. Was there a moment you did not know what to do? Where?
3. Was it too easy, too hard, or right? Which part?
4. Did you feel scared? When? Was it fun-scared or annoying-scared?
5. Could you always see what was going on? (too dark? too many things on screen?)
6. Shooting: did hitting someone feel good and clear? Did the weapons feel different from each other?
7. The "Lost" kids you wake by name: did you understand why? How did it make you feel?
8. Did the Kreyòl and the story feel real to you? Anything that sounded wrong or disrespectful?
9. What is the one thing you would change first?
10. Would you play it again, and would you tell a friend? (0 to 10)

### Kesyon yo an Kreyòl (BOUON: a native speaker must review before use)

1. Nan yon sèl fraz: kisa jwèt sa a ap rakonte?
2. Èske te gen yon moman ou pa t konnen kisa pou w fè? Kote?
3. Li te two fasil, two difisil, oswa jis kòrèk? Ki pati?
4. Èske ou te pè? Kilè? Se te yon pè ki amizan oswa ki anmède w?
5. Èske ou te toujou ka wè sa k ap pase? (two fènwa? twòp bagay sou ekran?)
6. Tire: èske li te santi bon lè w frape yon moun? Zam yo te santi diferan?
7. Timoun "Pèdi" yo ou reveye ak non yo: èske w konprann poukisa? Kijan sa fè w santi w?
8. Èske Kreyòl la ak istwa a te sanble vre pou ou? Gen bagay ki te mal oswa san respè?
9. Ki sèl bagay ou ta chanje an premye?
10. Èske w ta jwe l ankò, epi èske w ta di yon zanmi? (0 rive 10)
